Cell biology is the branch of biology that studies the structure, function, and behavior of cells, which are the basic units of life. Key concepts in cell biology include the cell theory, cell structure, cellular communication, and cellular metabolism. Cell biology provides insights into the mechanisms of various biological processes and is fundamental to understanding fundamental life sciences and medical research. It encompasses subdisciplines such as molecular biology, biochemistry, and genetics, and is crucial for fields such as biotechnology, pharmacology, and medicine.
